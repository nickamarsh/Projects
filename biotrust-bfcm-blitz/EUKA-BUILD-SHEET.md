## Why this is two contests

A Leaderboard on Euka's Contests page ranks creators by **one** metric, either posts or sales. The poster has one award of each kind, so build **Contest A** and **Contest B**. Contest A's link goes on the poster. If you want everything under one link, the other option is to ask support@euka.ai for a single Smart Contest.

## Contest A: "BioTRUST BFCM Blitz – Most Videos"

Go to Campaigns > Contests > New contest.

1. **General:** Type **Leaderboard Contest**. Rank by **Posts**. Content **Videos**. Products **All products**. No hashtag.
2. **Videos and Prizes:** Prize currency USD. Rank 1 = $1,000, rank 2 = $500, rank 3 = $250, one winner each.
3. **Customization:**
   - Name: `BioTRUST BFCM Blitz – Most Videos`. Banner: export it from the Canva poster.
   - Dates: Nov 20, 12:00 AM to Nov 30, 11:59 PM, **America/Los_Angeles**. You can't change these after creating the contest.
   - Custom link: `biotrust-bfcm-blitz`
   - Creative brief: https://canva.link/glvxiof6i2m9n0k
   - Disqualification rules:
     1. **Misleading claims (Auto).** Define it from the brief's AVOID list: "Says the product cures, treats, heals, fixes or reverses aging; promises results or timelines; gives medical advice or makes pregnancy/nursing safety claims."
     2. Spam or mass-produced content
     3. AI-generated content (Auto)
     4. Faceless content (Auto)
     5. Inadequate product demonstration (product and label not clearly shown)
     6. Minimum video length (Auto): 15 seconds
     7. Custom: "Bashes or makes negative claims about other brands"
   - Turn on **Disqualify videos already counted toward a retainer**.
   - **Brand terms → Upload PDF:** `BioTRUST-BFCM-Blitz-Contest-Terms.pdf`. The Google Doc is private (only you can open it), so a link would fail for creators.
4. **Payments:** **Pay via Euka**, the saved payment method, deadline **7 days** after the end.
5. **Notifications:**
   - Welcome message: "Welcome to the BioTRUST BFCM Blitz! Brief: https://canva.link/glvxiof6i2m9n0k · Join our Discord: https://discord.gg/sFV4uegZh9 · The $500 Top Seller prize is a separate contest. Join here too: [Contest B link]. Remember: no health claims."
   - Haven't-posted nudge after 2 days.
   - Ending soon reminder 2 days before the end (Nov 28).

## Contest B: "BioTRUST BFCM Blitz – Top Seller"

Open Contest A and click **Duplicate**, then change:
- Name `BioTRUST BFCM Blitz – Top Seller`, custom link `biotrust-bfcm-topseller`
- Rank by **Sales (GMV)**, a single rank: 1st = **$500**
- Dates, rules, brief, terms and payments stay the same. In the welcome message, link back to Contest A.

## Usage rights

Euka's help center says contests do **not** grant usage rights for ads. Two pieces cover this:

1. **When creators join:** the Brand terms above. They grant BioTRUST a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license for **1 year from the date each video is created** (on or after Nov 20, 2026), plus Spark codes on request.
2. **After the contest:** per-video requests sent from the Euka usage-rights template **"BFCM Blitz 2026 – Contest entry license (free, 1 year)"**. It is free, lasts 12 months, and has the terms PDF attached. Accepted videos go into Licensed Content. Send these from Dec 1: go to Content > Videos, filter to Nov 20–30, select the contest videos, click Request Usage and pick the template. Claude can also send them through the connector.
