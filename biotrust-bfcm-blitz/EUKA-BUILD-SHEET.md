## Why this is two contests

Euka's Contests page ranks a Leaderboard by **one** metric, either posts or sales. The poster has two different awards: the top 3 by number of videos, and $500 for top GMV. So build **Contest A** and **Contest B** below, and put Contest A's link on the poster.

Alternative: ask Euka support (support@euka.ai) to set this up as a single "Smart Contest" program, which can score both awards under one link. The MCP connector can't build a Smart Contest's scoring rule.

## Contest A: "BioTRUST BFCM Blitz – Most Videos"

Go to Campaigns > Contests > New contest.

1. **General:** Type **Leaderboard Contest**. Rank by **Posts**. Content **Videos**. Products **All products**. No hashtag.
2. **Videos and Prizes:** Prize currency USD. Rank 1 = $1,000, rank 2 = $500, rank 3 = $250, one winner each. Leaderboards have no max-budget option; the most this can pay is $1,750.
3. **Customization:**
   - Name: `BioTRUST BFCM Blitz – Most Videos`. Banner: export it from the Canva poster.
   - Dates: Nov 20, 12:00 AM to Nov 30, 11:59 PM, **America/Los_Angeles**. Leaderboard dates can't be changed after the contest is created, so double-check them.
   - Custom link: for example `biotrust-bfcm-blitz`.
   - Creative brief: **link the BioTRUST brief.** This account has no brief in Euka yet.
   - Disqualification rules (the poster's "no health claims" rule is enforced through Misleading claims):
     1. Misleading claims (Auto). Define it as: "Any health claim, meaning saying or implying the product treats, cures or prevents a disease or condition, or promising specific weight-loss or health results."
     2. Spam or mass-produced content
     3. AI-generated content (Auto)
     4. Faceless content (Auto)
     5. Inadequate product demonstration (product not clearly shown)
     6. Minimum video length (Auto). Recommended: 15 seconds. The default is 30.
     7. Custom: "Uses music that isn't cleared for commercial use"
   - Turn on **Disqualify videos already counted toward a retainer**.
   - **Brand terms → Upload PDF:** `BioTRUST-BFCM-Blitz-Content-License.pdf`. This is the usage-rights grant: creators can't join without accepting it.
4. **Payments:** **Pay via Euka**, a card or bank account, deadline **7 days** after the end (around Dec 7).
5. **Notifications:** Welcome message (include the Discord invite and Contest B's link), Haven't-posted nudge after 2 days, Ending soon reminder 2 days before the end (Nov 28).

## Contest B: "BioTRUST BFCM Blitz – Top Seller"

To combine the two in one link for creators, put Contest B's link in Contest A's welcome message and announcement, and on the poster next to the $500.

## Usage rights (done in Euka)

Euka's help center says contests **do not** grant usage rights for ads, so this uses two layers:

1. **When creators join:** the Brand terms PDF above. It grants a free, worldwide, perpetual license, plus Spark codes on request, and makes accepting it a condition of winning a prize.
2. **After the contest:** a per-video free usage-rights request, which puts each video in Licensed Content. **Already created in Euka:** a usage-rights template, *"BFCM Blitz 2026 – Contest entry license (free, perpetual)"*, with the PDF attached. From Dec 1, go to Content > Videos, filter to Nov 20–30, select the contest videos, click Request Usage and pick that template. Claude can also send these through the connector once the videos have synced.

Have counsel review the PDF and add BioTRUST's legal entity name and governing law before Nov 20.
